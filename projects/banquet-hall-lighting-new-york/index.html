Banquet Hall Lighting Project in New York

Lighting equipment supplied for a 900 m² banquet and wedding venue in New York, combining moving head lights and kinetic LED balls for different event settings.

900 m² · 141 Fixtures · 53 Kinetic Balls · 5.2 m Installation Height

Case Study

Project Story

The project was developed for a newly built banquet hall in New York. The venue needed a lighting setup that could improve the overall visual experience while supporting weddings, banquets, conferences and other events.

The Brief

Project Needs & Considerations

01

Flexible Event Use

The hall needed to support weddings, banquets, conferences and commercial events with different lighting atmospheres.

02

Dynamic Visual Effects

Moving head fixtures and kinetic LED balls were used to create different visual layers across the stage and ceiling.

03

Tight Project Schedule

Construction approval delayed the venue schedule while event bookings had already started, making equipment preparation and delivery time-sensitive.

Our Role

MOKA Scope

  • Equipment Selection

    Lighting fixtures were selected according to the venue size and required effects.

  • Lighting Equipment Supply

    MOKA supplied the moving head fixtures and kinetic LED ball system used in the project.

  • Installation Reference & Support

    Installation drawings and technical information were prepared before equipment arrived at the venue.

  • Remote Technical Support

    Remote assistance was provided during the installation and setup process.

The completed setup gives the banquet hall multiple visual options for weddings, conferences and other events. Moving head fixtures provide beam, wash and strobe effects, while the kinetic LED balls add movement and depth to the ceiling area.
