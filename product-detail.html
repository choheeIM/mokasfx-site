PULSE 1818 X6 – 18×18W 6-in-1 Flat LED PAR Light

Its slim 15 cm body suits limited-space setups. Product Overview

PULSE 1818 X6 is a 6-in-1 flat par light for stage color washes, venue ambience, and event lighting. Its RGBWA+UV LED system combines saturated colors, warm amber hues, and vivid UV accents in one fixture, giving more options for bars, nightclubs, live music venues, and event spaces.

This LED PAR light supports dimming, strobe, color jump, gradual, and pulse effects. These functions allow the fixture to move from soft background lighting to more rhythmic looks for live performances, DJ sets, parties, and other programmed event environments.

With RDM, DMX512, master-slave, sound-active, and auto modes, the PULSE 1818 X6 can be used in different control setups. The slim PAR light body is only 26 × 26 × 15 cm, while its 3.2 kg net weight helps simplify space planning for stage and venue lighting configurations.

  • 18 × 18W RGBWA+UV LEDs, for rich color washes, warm amber, and UV accents.
  • 25° / 40° optional beam angles, for flexible coverage for different layouts.
  • 10CH DMX with RDM / DMX512 support, for coordinated fixture control.
  • Dimming, strobe, color jump, gradual, and pulse effects, for varied lighting looks.
  • 15 cm slim body, suitable for limited-space installations.

FAQ

  1. 01

    What is the PULSE 1818 X6 flat par light used for?

    The PULSE 1818 X6 is designed for stage color washes and atmosphere lighting in bars, lounges, event spaces, and other entertainment environments.

  2. 02

    What type of LED PAR light is the PULSE 1818 X6?

    It is a 6-in-1 RGBWA+UV LED PAR light with 18 × 18W LEDs, producing saturated colors, warm amber tones, and UV accents.

  3. 03

    Which beam angle should I choose for the PULSE 1818 X6?

    The 25° option provides a tighter spread, while the 40° option covers a broader area. The suitable choice depends on fixture distance and the required coverage.

  4. 04

    How can this flat PAR light be controlled?

    The PULSE 1818 X6 supports RDM, DMX512, master-slave, sound-active, and auto modes. DMX operation uses 10 channels.

  5. 05

    How can I use this light?

    It can be mounted upside down or placed upright. It can also be wall-mounted, installed on a stand or base, or hung from a truss.
