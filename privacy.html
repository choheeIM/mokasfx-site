Privacy Policy

How MOKA LITE collects, uses and protects the information you submit through this website.

Last updated: 09,24,2026

Who We Are

This website is operated by MOKA LITE, a stage lighting solution and equipment supplier of stage lighting and event.

Website: https://mokalites.com

For privacy-related questions, please contact us at:

Email: info@mokalite.com
Address: 501, Building N, No. 46 Shangsheng East St, Baiyun District, Guangzhou, Guangdong, China 510440

Information We Collect

We collect personal information only when you voluntarily submit a form on our website.

Depending on the form, we may collect:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Company or organization name
  • Product or service interests
  • Inquiry subject and message
  • IP address
  • Browser and device information
  • Form submission date and time

We do not provide user registration or login functions, and we do not collect information through public comments or user profiles.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information submitted through forms to:

  • Respond to product or service inquiries
  • Provide quotations and business information
  • Contact you about your request
  • Provide customer support
  • Prevent spam, abuse and unauthorized submissions
  • Maintain appropriate business communication records

We do not sell your personal information.

Contact and Inquiry Forms

When you submit a contact or product inquiry form, your information may be sent to our authorized sales, customer service or technical staff.

The information may be transmitted through:

  • Our website hosting server
  • Our business email service
  • Our form processing or anti-spam service

These service providers may process your information only as necessary to operate the website, deliver emails, prevent abuse or respond to your inquiry.

Current service providers:

  • Hosting provider: siteground.com

Cookies and Technical Information

Our website may use essential cookies and technical technologies required for website operation, form submission, security and spam prevention.

These technologies may process information such as your IP address, browser type, device information and form submission details.

If analytics or marketing cookies are added in the future, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

Data Sharing

We may share submitted information with:

  • Authorized employees and representatives who handle inquiries
  • Our hosting provider
  • Our business email provider
  • Anti-spam or security service providers
  • Government authorities or professional advisers where required by law

We do not sell or rent personal information to third parties.

How Long We Keep Your Information

We keep form submissions only for as long as reasonably necessary to:

  • Respond to your inquiry
  • Complete related business communication
  • Maintain business records
  • Meet legal, accounting or security requirements

Contact form data retention period: 1 year

Email and inquiry records may be retained for 1 year, unless a longer period is required by law or necessary to resolve a dispute.

Your Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have the right to:

  • Request access to the information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your information
  • Withdraw consent for future communications
  • Object to or restrict certain processing activities

To make a request, contact us at info@mokalite.com. We may need to verify your identity before processing the request.

Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information submitted through our forms against unauthorized access, loss, misuse or disclosure.

However, no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version will always be published on this page with a revised “Last updated” date.
