Workflow

From project information to delivery and commissioning

  1. 01

    Project information

    You send stage dimensions, trim height, venue type, show dates, camera and power details.

  2. 02

    Design & quotation

    Zone plan, fixture schedule and an export-ready quotation — typically within a few working days.

  3. 03

    Production & testing

    Fixtures assembled, burned-in, addressed and labeled according to the plan.

  4. 04

    Packing & delivery

    Flight-case or export packaging with a packing and labeling plan matched to zones.

  5. 05

    Commissioning & support

    Remote commissioning support as standard; on-site support available according to region.
