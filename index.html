About MOKA

Professional Stage & SFX Lighting Manufacturer in Guangzhou

MOKA SFX LIGHT designs, engineers and manufactures professional moving heads, beam lights, wash lights, lasers, strobes, fog and confetti machines for concerts, clubs, theaters, weddings, broadcast and outdoor events. With an in-house R&D team, optical lab and 8,000 m² production floor, we ship OEM & ODM solutions to distributors, rental companies and project buyers in 60+ countries.
