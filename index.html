FAQ

Stage Lighting Solutions FAQ

Do you provide lighting design or only equipment?

We provide both. MOKA LITE supports lighting design, fixture selection, system configuration, equipment supply, and technical support.

What information is needed for a lighting solution?

Venue dimensions, stage size, mounting height, venue type, required effects, project schedule, drawings, and budget are helpful.

Can I purchase equipment without a complete lighting design?

Yes. Existing equipment lists and specifications can also be supplied directly.

What types of projects do you support?

Livehouses, events, clubs, banquet halls, wedding halls, nightclubs, and other entertainment lighting applications. Contact us if you have a stage lighting project or need a customized lighting solution.

How long does a typical project take?

A typical project takes about one month before delivery. The actual timeline depends on project complexity and approval speed.

How long do you provide after-sales support?

We provide one year of after-sales support for supplied lighting equipment.
