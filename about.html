Who We Are

Lighting for happiness

MOKA LITE is a professional stage lighting solution supplier and a stage lighting equipment supplier, serving indoor stages, nightclubs, lounge bars, banquet halls, hotels, and other entertainment venues worldwide.

From fixture selection to lighting design, from equipment supply to technical support, we help turn your project requirements into practical lighting systems — tailored to your space, atmosphere, visual goals, and budget.

Our goal is simple: bringing happiness to every space through lighting.
